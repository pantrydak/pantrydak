Pilot FAQs

Questions, answered

Ordering, payment, minimums and delivery for the Liverpool launch.

How do I place an order?
Send your shopping list to us on WhatsApp at +44 7768 651941 before Friday 8 PM. We'll confirm availability and your total.
How do I pay?
We accept instant UK Bank Transfer (Faster Payments) and card payments via a secure link.
Is there a minimum order?
The minimum order for weekend delivery is £20. Orders over £35 get free standard delivery (Extended Zone orders still pay the £2.00 distance fee).
When do I receive my delivery?
Deliveries take place every Saturday and Sunday. You'll receive your delivery time window by WhatsApp after the Friday 8 PM cutoff.
Which areas do you cover?
Standard Zone: L1–L8, L13 and L15. Extended Zone: L9–L12, L14, L16–L20, L25 and L28. Use the postcode checker to confirm.
